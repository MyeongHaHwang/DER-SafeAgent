Hint for chain-of-thought reasoning before producing JSON:

Think step by step. (i) Summarize the most recent change in telemetry vs the
prior window. (ii) Identify whether any event is anomalous given the protocol
and source. (iii) Hypothesize the attack class and which DER asset is implicated.
(iv) Estimate the energy-system consequence if no mitigation is applied.
(v) Pick the cheapest mitigation that prevents the consequence.

Do not include the steps in the final response -- only the final JSON.
